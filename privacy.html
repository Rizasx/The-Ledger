Privacy Policy

The Ledger Life — A Life, Kept

This app is a single-player life simulation game. This policy explains, plainly, what happens with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is collected, transmitted, or seen by us. The app does not have a server, does not create user accounts, and does not send your gameplay data anywhere.

What's stored on your device

All game progress — your character, their stats, and everything that happens during play — is saved locally on your own device, using standard on-device storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not visible to us or to any third party
  • Is only used to let you resume your game where you left off
  • Is deleted if you clear the app's storage/data, or uninstall the app

Third parties

The app loads a font from Google Fonts when you have an internet connection. Loading a web font can expose your IP address to Google in the same way any web request does. No gameplay data is included in that request. We don't use analytics, ad networks, or tracking of any kind.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach out through the app's listing page on the store where you downloaded it.
