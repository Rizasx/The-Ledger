What gets entered

Five columns, one life.

Every path is open from the same starting line — what you do with it is the only thing that decides how the story reads.

Career
Climb a real corporate ladder, join the force, break into music or film, or run your own movie studio — with genuine risk of a blockbuster or a disaster.
Wealth
Invest in stocks and crypto, hire a broker, build a property empire, or launder money through an account no one can seize.
Family
Date, marry, raise children, keep friends or make enemies. Every relationship is its own thread, and every thread has weight.
Power
Run for office, build a party, campaign for real, and — if you make it far enough — hold the presidency itself.
Risk
Turn to crime and build a family of your own — recruit a crew, take territory, and live with the constant chance it all falls apart.
